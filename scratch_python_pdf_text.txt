Section 4 — Ratio & Mixture
Rice and dal are mixed in the ratio 7:3. If 8 kg of the mixture is removed and replaced by 8 kg of dal, the ratio
becomes 7:5. Find the original quantity of the mixture.
Solution: In 8 kg removed, rice=5.6 kg and dal=2.4 kg. After replacing it with 8 kg of dal:
Rice = 0.7T − 5.6
Dal = 0.3T − 2.4 + 8 = 0.3T + 5.6
Set the new ratio to 7:5:
(0.7T − 5.6)/(0.3T + 5.6) = 7/5
5(0.7T − 5.6) = 7(0.3T + 5.6)
3.5T − 28 = 2.1T + 39.2
1.4T = 67.2
T = 48 kg
Answer: 8
